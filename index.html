About Me

I started working at MSI as an undergraduate before pursuing my PhD in Scientific Computation at the University of Minnesota. After serving as an analyst in the LM&P bioinformatics group focusing on clinical pipelines development, I joined the SCS group in early 2020 as a Scientific Computing Consultant.

My current work and research interests center around Machine Learning, Deep Learning, neural network architectures, and distributed training methodologies.
